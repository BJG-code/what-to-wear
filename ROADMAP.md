# Roadmap

The full detail is in [docs/spec.md](docs/spec.md).

## Version 1.0 (current)

- Running and cycling, planned up to 5 days ahead for a chosen day and start time
- "Getting to know you" profile: name, and runs hot / neutral / runs cold
- Forecast from Open-Meteo for the phone's location, or a town you search for (central London if location isn't available)
- Dressing temperature from feels like, effort, distance, profile and what you've taught it, with the working shown
- Outfit in three cards (Upper body, Lower body, Accessories), with notes when they apply, including weather changing during the session
- Too hot / Perfect / Too cold after each session, tuning future outfits separately for running and cycling
- Settings: profile, learned offsets with Reset, and rules of thumb you can adjust
- A home-screen web app on the iPhone, hosted free on GitHub Pages; everything personal stays on the phone

**Done when:** installed on the iPhone by Sun 22 Nov 2026, used before real runs and rides, and rated "Perfect" on most sessions after two weeks of feedback.

## Ideas for later versions (v1.1 and beyond)

- Smarter learning from the saved ratings
- More activities: walking and hiking
- A list of saved locations, and route-based forecasts
- A second weather source (Met Office or Apple WeatherKit)
- Heart rate and workouts from Apple Health, Strava or Garmin
- An AI-written summary from Claude (costs per use)
- Reminders, widgets, Apple Watch
- Accounts and syncing between devices
- A native iPhone app (needs a personal Mac)
