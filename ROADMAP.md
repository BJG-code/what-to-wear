# Roadmap

## Version 1 (current)

- Running and cycling
- Uses your phone's location first, with the option to search for another place (e.g. on holiday)
- A clean, clear list of what to wear, from head to toe
- Personal preference: feel the cold / feel normal / run warm
- Remembers your activity, preference and any place you searched for on your device

## Ideas for later versions

- More activities: walking and hiking
- Show the name of the town for your current location, not just "Your current location"
- Plan ahead: pick a time later today or tomorrow morning, not just right now
